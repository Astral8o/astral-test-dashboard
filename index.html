Good to know

Before you book.

How do I book?
Tap any “Add” or “Book Now” button, choose your services and send your request. It goes straight to the studio, and we'll reply to confirm a time and the deposit details.
Do you take a deposit?
Yes, a 50% deposit secures your appointment and comes off your total on the day. It's non-refundable for no-shows.
What if I'm running late?
A few minutes is fine, just message us. Past 15 minutes we may need to shorten or reschedule so the next client isn't kept waiting.
How long do lashes last?
Two to three weeks with proper care. We recommend a refill every two weeks to keep them looking full.
